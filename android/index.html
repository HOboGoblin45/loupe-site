How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android

    Do this on your Android phone. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.

  2. Open the downloaded file

    Tap the download notification, or open your Files app and find Loupe-android.apk in Downloads.

  3. Allow the install

    The first time, Android asks for permission. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

  4. Tap Install, then Open

    If Play Protect says it hasn't seen the app before, tap More details, then Install anyway. Loupe isn't on Google Play yet, so Android checks with you first.

Questions

Good to know

Why isn't Loupe on Google Play?

It's on the way. Until then, this download is the same app, signed by Loupe. Android shows extra warnings for any app installed from outside Google Play, which is why it asks you to confirm.

How do I update?

Most updates arrive inside the app automatically. If a bigger update needs a new download, come back to this page and install it the same way. It installs over the old version and keeps your Dresser and likes.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. Loupe is free on the App Store.

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone running Android 7.0 or later with a 64-bit processor, which covers every Android phone sold in recent years.
