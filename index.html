Your Dresser

A wardrobe you curate, not a cart you forget.

Every piece you save lands in your Dresser — sorted, searchable, and synced. Loupe keeps watching after you save: when a piece goes on sale or your size comes back, you’ll know.

  • —Save with a swipe. Anything you love, one gesture away.
  • —Price-drop & restock alerts. One gentle daily digest — never spam.
  • —Your sizes, up front. Available sizes right on every tile.

Look Builder

Style whole looks — or let AI do it.

Drag your saved pieces onto a canvas and build the outfit before you buy it. Or tap Style Me and Loupe’s AI composes a full look from your taste — head to toe, from the whole catalog.

  • —Build it yourself. Drag, resize, layer — like a moodboard that shops.
  • —Style Me. AI assembles looks from pieces you love. Tap to reshuffle.
  • —Share it. Send a look to a friend — every piece stays shoppable.
Style DNA

See your taste, distilled.

The more you swipe, the more Loupe understands you. Your Style DNA reveals your signature — top brands, palette, silhouettes, price sweet spot — in one shareable card. Some tastes are rarer than others; find out how rare yours is.

  • —Your aesthetic, named and visualized.
  • —Top brands & colors at a glance.
  • —One tap to shop your whole vibe.
Make it yours

Style is better with friends.

Claim a @username, share your Dresser and Looks, and send pieces straight to friends in chat — with reactions, so you actually know what they think. You decide exactly what’s public.

  • —Send the piece, not a screenshot. Chat about it right in Loupe.
  • —Share your style. Your Dresser, Looks and Likes — if you choose.
  • —Private by default. Nothing’s shared until you turn it on.
Made to fit you

Set your budget, sizes, colors and silhouettes. Loupe shows pieces that fit your life — and if we’re missing a label you love, add it yourself and it joins your feed.

  • —Your budget. Set a range and stick to it.
  • —Add your own brand. Bring any label you love into your feed.
